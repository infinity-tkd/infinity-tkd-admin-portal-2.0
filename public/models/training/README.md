# Training anatomy assets

These bundles are generated by `node scripts/build-training-anatomy.mjs` from the original atlas files in `public/models`.

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.
Source and licence: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html

The training bundles retain the muscular, skeletal and connective-tissue structures and their original identifiers. The reference tier repacks the original geometry without altering it. The compact tier simplifies geometry with locked boundaries and a maximum additional relative mesh error of 0.003. It is an educational reference, not a scan of an individual student.

Adapted atlas renderer: Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag, MIT licence. The original notice is retained at `CloneIntegrate/human-atlas-main/LICENSE`.

The portal supplies its own training context, saved material relationships, learning coverage, controls and interface. No student information is embedded in these public assets.
