# Improvement ideas for review

Infinity Taekwondo · 1 November 2026

These are proposals for the academy to review, not an implementation plan that has already been approved. The current role fixes are documented separately in the [role access review](ROLE_ACCESS_REVIEW_2026-11-01.md). Existing calendar, notification, operational health and student-data features are not listed again as new work.

Effort is a relative engineering estimate: **small** is a contained change; **medium** affects several parts of the application; **large** needs a policy decision and coordinated database, API and interface changes. Exact scope and release timing should follow the academy's selections.

| Priority | Idea | Benefit and proposed behavior | Effort | Decision or dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Explain effective access and preview permission changes | Show why a user can or cannot perform an action: owner authority, user override, role override, inherited default and required page. Before saving a matrix change, show affected roles/accounts and features that will become unavailable. This makes access reviews easier and reduces accidental lockouts. | Medium | Use the shared evaluator; restrict account-level explanations and change previews to the appropriate administrator. Decide the information ordinary staff may see about their own access. |
| 2 | Separate account administration from academy configuration | Replace the shared staff-management write key with narrower account-management and academy-settings permissions. An administrator could manage branding or renewal timing without receiving account/role authority, or manage staff accounts without changing financial policy. | Medium | Approve the intended role matrix and migration rules first. Existing explicit denials must remain denials; do not automatically widen access while converting old grants. |
| 3 | Add finer privacy controls and sensitive-access audit | Build on this review's restricted full-record reads and operational summaries. Within Directory and Staff, separate access to medical notes, emergency contacts, addresses and other sensitive categories where the academy needs it. Record sensitive-record access without copying field values into the audit log. | Large | Agree which roles and workflows need each data category, retention and emergency access. Enforce the decision in database/API boundaries; hiding a field in the interface is insufficient. |
| 4 | Maintain approved staging accounts for every role | Provide a controlled test environment with separate Root, Admin and coaching-role accounts, MFA coverage and fictional fixtures. Run sign-in, permission-denial, session-revocation and desktop/mobile release checks before deploying. This closes the gap where a role has no real test account available. | Medium | Choose the staging environment, approved account owners, MFA recovery process and reset procedure. Keep production student records and credentials out of the fixtures. |
| 5 | Scope coach access by branch and class | Limit student lists and actions to assigned branches/classes where appropriate, with a clear substitute-coach path. Distinguish access to a current roster from access to historical records after an enrollment or coaching assignment ends. | Large | Approve the scope rules, historical access, cross-branch events and emergency exceptions. Update queries, transactions, exports and background jobs together. |
| 6 | Add temporary, audited delegation | Let an authorized owner grant a limited capability for a defined period, with a reason, approver, scope and expiry. Useful for substitute coaches, a short financial handover or event coordination. Expired authority must stop working even in an already-open session. | Medium–large | Decide who can approve, maximum duration and which actions cannot be delegated. Apply expiry in the server/database evaluator, not only through a scheduled cleanup. |
| 7 | Rehearse restoration and define operational alerts | Build on the existing job-health view with an agreed restore exercise, recovery runbook and meaningful alerts for failed jobs, growing delivery backlogs or stale data. A successful backup should be followed by proof that a usable copy can be restored in isolation. | Medium | Set recovery objectives, alert recipients and escalation rules. Use an isolated restore target and obtain approval before any exercise that could affect production. |
| 8 | Add consent-aware contact previews with manual sending | Before a membership or attendance follow-up, preview the intended recipient, approved contact channel and message using the current verified record. Respect consent and contact preferences, and retain an outcome note after the staff member sends or cancels it. | Medium | Agree approved wording, permitted contact recipients, consent and audit requirements. Keep the final send deliberate; do not introduce automatic messages to students or contacts by default. |

## Suggested order

Start with access explanations and staging-role acceptance because they make later changes easier to review and verify. Approve the narrower privileged permissions and personal-field policy before expanding delegation or coach scope. Restoration exercises and contact previews can then follow the academy's operational priorities.

No new permission keys, access scopes, delegations, staging accounts, message sends or backup restores are created by this list. Credential rotation and a full separate student-portal build remain deferred rather than being added back into the proposed work.
